Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in San Diego

Many mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

Minor issues don't stay minor when they're ignored. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a few miserable nights.
